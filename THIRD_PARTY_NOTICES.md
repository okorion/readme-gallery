# Third-party components

| Component | Version | License | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three) | 0.186.1 | MIT | Rendering, RoundedBoxGeometry, RoomEnvironment |
| [Playwright](https://github.com/microsoft/playwright) | 1.64.1 | Apache-2.0 | Chromium capture and image generation |
| [Pretendard](https://github.com/orioncactus/pretendard/releases/tag/v1.3.9) | 1.3.9 | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | Bundled, unmodified variable webfont |

The font license is included at [assets/fonts/OFL.txt](assets/fonts/OFL.txt).
Three.js and Playwright are installed from the lockfile; their npm packages include
their license texts. Chromium is downloaded separately by Playwright and retains
its own notices. No external 3D model assets are bundled: the exhibition geometry
is generated by the gallery code.
